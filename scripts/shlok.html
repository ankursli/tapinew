The Tapi Aarti Shlok
ॐ तापी नमस्तुभ्यं सूर्यपुत्री नमो঍स्तु ते।
पापं हर मम देवि पावने जलदायिनि।।
Meaning
O Tapi, daughter of the Sun God, we bow to you. Purifier and giver of life-giving water, wash away our sins and bless all who gather at your sacred banks. May your waters flow pure and eternal, cooling the earth and awakening devotion in every heart.
Sandhya Aarti
जय तापी माता, सूर्य की दुलारी ।
तेरे तट पर संध्या, जलती ज्योत हजारी ।।
तापी नमस्तुभ्यम्
Preserving the sacred Tapi • Surat, Gujarat
